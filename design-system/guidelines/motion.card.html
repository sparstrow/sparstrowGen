Four animations, plain CSS keyframes, no framer-motion. Everything is opacity and transform only. Durations sit between 150ms and 200ms — fast enough to read as state feedback rather than choreography, which is the app’s whole register.
There are no motion tokens. Durations and curves are written literally at each call site. That means §7 of the doctrine cannot be changed in one place today, and the four values above are the real vocabulary any new animation should match. The previous design system papered over this by inventing --transition-base: 140ms ease, a token present in no stylesheet and consumed by nothing — doc/KnownGaps.md G-18. It is deliberately absent here.
Not verified: prefers-reduced-motion. Nothing in globals.css responds to it, so spg-pulse in particular — infinite, and the one users notice — currently runs regardless of the setting.
