Four steps, one decision. All of them come from --radius: 0.625rem via calc() in globals.css — they are not independently chosen. Moving that one base moves the whole scale together, which is why a literal pixel radius anywhere is a defect rather than a shortcut: it pins one corner while every adjacent corner moves.
The relationship is deliberately kept as calc() in tokens/spacing.css rather than resolved to 6 / 8 / 10 / 14px. Mirroring the output would record the numbers and lose the rule that produced them.
There is no --radius-full. Pills and avatars use Tailwind's rounded-full directly. The previous design system defined one in its own token file and drew it on this card, which made the scale look like five steps the app could reproduce — it has four.
