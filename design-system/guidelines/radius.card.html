Derived
All five come from --radius: 0.625rem in globals.css via calc() — they are not independently chosen. Changing that one base moves the whole scale together, which is the intended behaviour and the reason not to hardcode a pixel radius anywhere. Mismatched corners show faster than almost any other inconsistency, because the elements sit adjacent.
