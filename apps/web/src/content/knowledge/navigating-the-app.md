The app is built for keeping several things open at once. Here's how to move around quickly.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar groups every page under three headers:

- **Personal** — Chat, Inbox, Task Board, Memory.
- **Workspace** — Agents, Teams, Projects, Runs, Machines, Pipelines, Schedule, Imports.
- **Configure** — Skills, Terminals, Knowledge Center, Settings.

The Dashboard sits above the groups. The **workspace switcher** at the very top names the
workspace you're in and holds your profile and workspace links. If you have more than one
workspace — a personal one and a work one, say — they're listed there and switching is one
click; the whole app reloads into that workspace's own projects, chats and machines. With
only one workspace the list isn't shown, since there'd be no choice to make.

The sidebar-toggle button (in the tab bar) collapses the sidebar to an icon-only rail when
you want more room; click it again to expand.

### Pinned items

The **Pinned** section keeps shortcuts to the things you touch most. Pin a project or run from
its row's ⋯ menu and it appears here; drag pinned items to reorder them. Pins persist between
sessions.

## Workspace tabs

Above the header is a **tab bar**. Each tab is an independent view into the app, like a browser:

- **+** opens a new tab.
- **×** on a tab closes it (the neighbouring tab takes over).
- Navigating inside a tab updates just that tab — so you can leave the Task Board open in one
  tab while digging through a run in another.
- **Back / forward** arrows move through that tab's history.

## Command palette

Press **Ctrl + K** (**⌘ + K** on Mac), or click **Search** in the sidebar, to open the command
palette. Start typing to jump straight to any page, agent, team, or project — no clicking
through menus.

## Breadcrumbs and status

The header shows a **breadcrumb** trail for where you are (e.g. *Projects / My App*), so nested
detail pages always tell you the path back. To its right:

- A **live / offline** badge — whether the app is connected to the local core service.
- A **waiting** badge when items need your attention (it links to the Dashboard queue).

## On a narrow screen

Below tablet width the sidebar collapses into a drawer. Tap the menu button to slide it open
over a dimmed backdrop; picking a destination closes it automatically.
